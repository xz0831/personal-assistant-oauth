Privacy Policy

Personal Assistant (the "app") is a private tool used by one person, its owner, with their own Google account. This page says what Google data the app uses and what happens to it. Last updated: 23 October 2026.

Who uses the app

Only the owner. The app is not offered to the public, has no other users and runs no service for others. The owner signs in with their own Google account and grants access themselves.

Google data the app can access

Only what the owner grants on Google's consent screen: Gmail (read, modify and send), Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Docs, and Google Contacts (read only).

How the data is used

  • Only to carry out a task the owner asks for, such as finding an email, summarizing a document, adding a calendar event or sending a message the owner wrote or approved.
  • Not for advertising, not for profiling, not to train AI models of the app's own, and not for any purpose the owner did not ask for.

Where the data goes

  • The app runs on the owner's own computers and talks to Google through the official Google APIs.
  • When a task the owner gives uses an AI assistant, the parts of the data that the task needs can be sent to the AI model provider the owner uses for that task (for example OpenAI or Anthropic), under that provider's own terms. Nothing is sent to an AI provider without a task from the owner.
  • The data is not sold or rented. It may also be sent to recipients or services the owner selects when a task requires it, such as sending an email or reporting a result through the owner's chosen chat service.

Storage and protection

  • The integration runs in the owner's local assistant environment, not as a public service. Task results and relevant Google data may remain in local files, assistant conversation history, or the owner's selected AI and chat services. Retention depends on those systems and the owner's settings.
  • The sign-in tokens Google issues are kept in a local file on the owner's computer that only the owner's user account can read. They are not posted, logged or sent to anyone other than Google.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • The owner can remove the app's access at any time at Google Account: third-party connections.
  • To stop future authorization, revoke the app in Google Account settings and remove its local token file. Revocation does not delete previously saved task results or conversation history. The owner must delete those copies in the relevant local files, assistant history, and selected external services.

Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app, or reach the owner through github.com/xz0831.
