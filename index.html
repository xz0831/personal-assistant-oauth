Personal Assistant

Personal Assistant is a private tool used by one person, its owner, to work with their own Google account from their own computers. It is not offered to anyone else and has no other users.

What it does

When the owner asks for a task, the tool reads or changes the owner's own Google data through the official Google APIs, for example:

  • reading, searching and sending the owner's Gmail messages,
  • reading and editing the owner's Google Calendar events,
  • finding and reading the owner's Google Drive files, Sheets and Docs,
  • reading the owner's Google Contacts.

It acts only on the owner's request and only on the owner's own account.
